# MathGraph Crystal V0

This branch is a deliberately small representation experiment. It does not claim a universal ontology or new truth authority.

The goal is to test whether one domain-agnostic representation can replay structural laws already qualified elsewhere in the programme:

1. consequence-relative quotienting and quotient falsification;
2. erasure of raw representation differences that do not change protected consequence;
3. alternative repair routes with conjunctive warrant/capability support;
4. revocation-sensitive reclosure;
5. mutable consequential state and greatest robust viability;
6. action quotienting by induced protected transition.

The representation is intentionally thin. Future/protected signature determines state-class identity on a declared boundary. Hyperedge represents a typed capability or repair route. support_refs are opaque references to external authority, not authority created by MathGraph. ResidualPair witnesses an invalid quotient. Residual is what remains after live reclosure. Viability is computed on the stateful transition system rather than inferred from static cover alone.

## Epistemic boundary

Passing this V0 gate means only that the same tiny executable schema can express these already-understood finite patterns without adding math-, Lean-, ARC-, or Metatron-specific coordinates.

It does not prove that the schema is globally minimal or universally sufficient. Any later same-representation/different-protected-consequence witness must split or refine the representation.

## Lineage

The V0 tests are executable distillations of previously qualified programme patterns recorded in Research OS, including the MSI/Metatron quotient-sufficiency falsifier, G23Q representation quotient, Metatron Warranted Continuation Viability V4-V7, ARC protected observation/action quotient results, and CLC/Metatron support/provenance/revocation separation.

Exact source/run hashes remain in Research OS and their source repositories. This branch does not duplicate those authorities; it tests a common representation layer over them.
