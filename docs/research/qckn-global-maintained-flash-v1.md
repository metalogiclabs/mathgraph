## Controls

### ISOLATED

All verified source events are admitted, but no bridge certificates exist.

The circuit, embodied, and opaque targets finish cold.

### SHAM_BRIDGE

Three same-shaped bridges are admitted with wrong source keys.

### NO_REVOCATION

Cross-domain bridges remain valid, but the arithmetic old two-class quotient is kept after scope expansion.

The arithmetic ACTIVE state is allowed to coarsen but raw reserve is discarded.

### RESTART

After Wave 1, the runtime snapshot is canonicalized and reconstructed. The event bus is rebuilt from declarative contracts and already-admitted events. Continuing closure must produce the exact same final digest as the uninterrupted arm.

## Boundary

It does **not** establish arbitrary semantic transfer among ARC, Collatz, Lean, circuit complexity, or theorem proving. Cross-domain effects remain bridge-certified, and typed costs remain incomparable without an explicit conversion contract.
