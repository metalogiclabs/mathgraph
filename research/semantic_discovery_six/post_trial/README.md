The two isolated adaptive investigators reached the same supported final conclusions. Neither locked final contains an unsupported decisive warrant. This is a small operationally held-out pilot, not evidence of a unique MathGraph architectural advantage.

The locked summaries recorded 72/79 credits before two subsequent clerical requests added five credits per arm. Neither final changed. The mixed cost observations establish no overall winner: MathGraph used fewer artificial credits but more code and elapsed time. Complete preparation, model token and monetary costs were not measured. Both investigators used the supplied warrant resolver, so this is not a resolver ablation.

Selection used populations of 256 YAML, 122 aerospace and two mathematical-logic examples. The two YAML cases are closely related; the mathematical examples are Boolean MaxSMT. The experiment does not cover six independent failure families or establish general natural-language correspondence. Isolation was procedural rather than a hardened sandbox; prior model training exposure is unknown.
