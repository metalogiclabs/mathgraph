# DARK-GS real camera/gyro: post-first-turn angular replay and honest recognition boundary

Existing v2 original source remains frozen: \`darkgs_column_v2.py\` Git blob \`2a00bf9ec471a0d03edfe85d1622cb78b1c8efb7\`. Original author publishes two aircraft-mounted RGB-camera/filtered-gyro files in Dataverse, doi:10.57745/EV12AZ, names \`360.zip\` and \`360_2.zip\`, exact MD5 + SHA256 + message counts in source. Earlier independent [Actions 40036880680](https://github.com/metalogiclabs/mathgraph/actions/runs/40036880680) found the exposed \`360\` integrated 1.0143 gyro turns and 150 camera frames after a nominal first-turn, whereas \`360_2\` integrated only 0.9892 turns (no later frames). Source publisher pose admission separately rejected both as evidence of TWO uninterrupted body-Z yaw rotations, and the external authenticity of the pose topic remains unverified. Therefore even successful matching is not a flight-worthy heading result.

New falsifier: With *unchanged* source descriptor, reproduce gyro-integrated first-turn frame partition and quarantine >2-Hamming contradictory cells, freeze reference before observing later images, and quantify how many DISTINCT angular bins are actually revisited by the 'post-firstturn' images. A single partially revisited arc cannot be called a second revolution. For later frame, evaluate two same-data conventional baselines: full unthresholded normalized grayscale pixel-column cosine similarity against every admitted firstturn reference; and 16-bit Hamming nearest neighbor. Report cosine top-score, next-best margin, #within 0.02 of top, Hamming best tie count, nominal gyro matching-cell presence (only for offline self-consistency, not independent heading truth). No thresholds fitted on second recording and no postfirstturn image can update the initial frozen map.

Critical guards: unique publisher zip MD5+SHA256, unique image+gyro ROS connections, original message counts, strictly increasing source-native headers, source clock discontinuity and out-of-domain images preserved, no odometry or pose decoded, no true gyro/camera time-bound, no genuine second complete gyro turn, no independent camera pose and no safety claim. The flag 'navigation correction authorized' is FALSE for all possible results; when model fails no exception suppressed into apparent source success. This is an honest descriptive measurement of real images on an aircraft, not a demonstration of corrected heading.
